Engineering Focus

The Field Engineering Discipline

System integration is a hands-on, practical engineering trade. Our technicians and engineers work directly on site across South India—pulling structured cabling through conduits, configuring IP cameras and managed switches in server racks, programming PBX exchanges, and verifying biometric door strikes.

We value technical curiosity, methodical workmanship, neat physical cabling habits, and a respect for client data security. Every project offers exposure to real enterprise hardware and the opportunity to build foundational integration expertise.

Hands-On Field Work Real-world hardware deployment across campuses and corporate buildings.
Continuity Standards Training in structured configuration backups, labeling, and restoration.
Career Pathways

Representative Integration Disciplines

Illustrative job categories that support our ongoing integration and maintenance operations across South India.

Surveillance Integration Specialist

Field Technical

Responsible for physical CCTV camera positioning, lens focal length adjustments, NVR setup, channel licensing, and ensuring optimal video coverage of perimeters, gates, and corridors.

  • • Focus on IP/Analog camera optics & night vision adjustments
  • • NVR configuration, RAID arrays, and scheduled recording profiles
  • • Video feed testing and client monitoring console setup

Network Cabling & Switch Engineer

Infrastructure

Specializes in Cat6/fiber structured cabling, server rack assembly, patch panel termination, Layer 2/3 switch configuration, VLAN tagging, and enterprise Wi-Fi deployment.

  • • Neat rack cable dressing, wire management, and port labeling
  • • Managed switch provisioning and PoE load distribution
  • • Signal testing, continuity audits, and Wi-Fi coverage validation

EPABX & Voice Telecom Technician

Telephony

Installs and maintains analog, digital, and IP-PBX communication hardware, MDF distribution frames, intercom stations, and direct station selection executive consoles.

  • • Extension routing, trunk line testing, and dial plan setup
  • • Auto-attendant, IVR voice prompt, and voicemail integration
  • • Audio continuity and line noise troubleshooting

Systems Support & Backup Coordinator

Continuity

Oversees preventative maintenance schedules, creates and validates software/system backups for clients, conducts periodic health checks, and handles component replacements.

  • • Scheduled backup extraction for switch configs, NVRs, and PBX
  • • Biometric database verification and time-attendance sync audits
  • • Rapid restore assistance during client hardware replacement
