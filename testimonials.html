Quality Assurance

The Project Handover Protocol

Every deployment goes through structured verification before sign-off to ensure complete institutional satisfaction.

1. Operational Verification

All camera streams are confirmed for field of view and resolution; all network drops are continuity-tested; PBX extensions are audited for clean audio and routing.

2. Complete System Documentation

Port mapping charts, cable identification tags, administrative IP addresses, and user guides are delivered directly to the client's administrative team.

3. Baseline Configuration Archive

Full software configurations of switches, NVRs, PBX boards, and biometric systems are backed up and archived securely for rapid future restoration.
