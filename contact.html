Integration & Service Questions

Answers to common questions about our system integration methodology, software backups, and regional deployments.

Timelines depend on campus acreage, conduit readiness, and the number of physical endpoints. A typical school or mid-sized commercial office deployment (30–60 cameras and structured networking) typically takes 7 to 14 working days, including cabling, rack dressing, testing, and backup archiving.

In technology integration, hardware is easily replaced, but complex switch VLAN configurations, PBX dial plans, camera stream profiles, and biometric databases take days to reconstruct if lost. By archiving configuration baselines, we guarantee rapid recovery if any hardware component experiences power failure or accidental resets.

Nevus Infocom is an engineering and system integration firm, not a consumer retail shop. We supply cameras, switches, PBX units, and biometric devices specifically to deliver turnkey client projects where we also handle structured cabling, mounting, and configuration.

We serve institutions, businesses, and government bodies across Karnataka, Tamil Nadu, Kerala, Andhra Pradesh, and Telangana. Our field technicians coordinate on-site inspections and installation schedules directly with client facility managers.

We offer scheduled preventative maintenance visits to clean optical dome covers, vacuum server rack ventilation filters, test battery backup switchovers, audit NVR drive health, and re-verify that software configuration backups match the latest facility state.
